## 3. What the measurements are, and are not

This section matters more than the rest of this document, so it is written
plainly.

**Every measurement is an estimate with an error band.** A speed is reported as
a value plus or minus a band — "128.4 ± 4.0 km/h" — and the band is the
measurement error, not a rounding hint. A value read without its band is being
read as more certain than it is. The app never displays one without the other,
and neither should you.

**Some readings are marked low confidence.** Those are excluded from trends and
never feed an insight. They remain visible because hiding them would be its own
distortion. Do not average them in.

**Some readings are not measurements at all.** Where the app has used its
simulated engine rather than a real capture and pose pass, that is recorded on
the session and shown in the app and in any export. Those numbers are
synthesised and mean nothing about how fast anyone bowled.

**The error band has not yet been validated against radar.** Until that study is
complete (#33), no accuracy claim should be relied upon, and none is made.

**Estimated gains are predictions, not promises.** Where the app says a change
to your action may be worth a range of km/h, that is a modelled estimate derived
from published research, not a guarantee of a result for you.

## 4. Workload guidance is not medical advice

The workload ledger implements bowling-load guidelines intended to reduce injury
risk, particularly lumbar stress injuries in fast bowlers. It is guidance, and
it is free on every tier because it should never be behind a payment.

It is **not** medical advice, physiotherapy, or a diagnosis. It does not know
about your body, your history, or your injuries. It cannot see that you are
already sore. If you are in pain, or returning from injury, see a qualified
physiotherapist or doctor — not an app.

The figures currently in the app are flagged as illustrative and have not been
sourced to a specific governing-body directive. That flag appears in the app
and it is accurate: treat the numbers as indicative until it is removed.
