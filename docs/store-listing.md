# Play store listing copy — draft

Issues #64 and #66. Written in the product's voice: sentence case, British
English, second person, no emoji, no exclamation marks. The design system's
counter-example — "Unleash your inner express pace" — is the thing to stay
furthest from.

**Constraint that shapes all of it:** no accuracy claim the radar validation
(#33) cannot yet support. Nothing below states a speed accuracy figure, because
none has been measured. When it has, this copy should be revisited to say so —
a measured band is a selling point, and a vague one is a liability.

**Second constraint:** the listing declares an 18+ target audience (#46), so
nothing here may read as addressed to juniors, and no screenshot may show one.

## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Sightscreen measures a fast bowler's action from a single phone video and tells
you the one thing to change to bowl quicker.

Put your phone on a tripod side-on to the pitch, bowl your spell, and walk back
to the phone knowing what happened. No wearables, no coach with a stopwatch, no
studio.

WHAT YOU GET

Ball speed for every delivery, with its error band. Not a number pretending to
be certain — a measurement that shows how sure it is. If a reading is
unreliable, Sightscreen says so and keeps it out of your trend.

One thing to change. Not a dashboard of forty metrics you have to interpret
yourself. Sightscreen looks at your front knee at release, your run-up speed,
your arm delay and your trunk flexion, and names the single biggest opportunity
in your action — with the evidence, and the research behind it.

A drill for it. Short, specific, and set up so your next session checks whether
it worked.

A workload ledger. Every ball you bowl, logged against the guideline for your
age, so you can see when you are close to the limit. This is free. It is free on
every tier, it is free forever, and it will never be behind a payment.

HOW IT WORKS IN A NET

The recording screen is built to be read from twenty metres, in sunlight. Full
screen colour, a delivery count in the largest type the screen allows, and
nothing else. Every state change also makes a sound, so the session works when
you are walking back to your mark and not looking at the phone.

Placement checks run before you bowl — landscape, level, crease and stumps in
frame, enough light for slow motion. If you override one, every delivery in that
session is marked low confidence. You can override it. You cannot be kept in the
dark about what it costs.

YOUR VIDEO STAYS ON YOUR PHONE

Capture, analysis and your whole history run on the device. Your footage is not
uploaded. There is no account to create before your first session, and the app
works in aeroplane mode.

You can export everything you have as CSV whenever you want, and delete
everything permanently in one action.

FREE AND PRO

Free: three analysed sessions a month, your complete speed log, and the entire
workload ledger.

Pro: unlimited analysis, the full metric breakdown, retest comparisons, trends
and the drill library.

OPEN TESTING

Sightscreen is in open testing and the measurements are still being validated
against radar. Speeds are shown with error bands, and where a reading comes from
a development build rather than a real capture, the app says so. If a number
looks wrong, tell us — that is what this phase is for.

Sightscreen is for bowlers aged 18 and over.
```

Roughly 2,400 characters, leaving room.

## Beta expectations (open testing description)

```
This is an early build. Capture, review, the improve loop and the workload
ledger all work. Speed and angle measurements are still being validated against
radar, so treat the numbers as indicative and read the error bands.

What we most want to hear about: capture that failed and cost you a spell, a
speed that looked obviously wrong, and anything you could not read at twenty
metres.
```
